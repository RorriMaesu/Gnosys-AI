## Module 1: Immunity & Respiration
*Goal: Master lymphatic defense loops and respiratory gas transport physics.*

### Lesson 3.1: The Lymphatic System & Immunity
* **Concept:** Lymphatic vessels, lymph nodes, thymus, spleen. Innate immunity (barrier defenses, phagocytes, natural killer [NK] cells, interferon, complement). Adaptive immunity: cell-mediated (T-cells: helper CD4+, cytotoxic CD8+; antigen presentation via MHC-I/II) vs. humoral immunity (B-cells: activation, plasma cells, antibody structures, clonal selection).
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Simulating the immune response to a viral profile by coordinating macrophages, NK cells, and cytotoxic T-cell attacks, or analyzing lymphadenopathy in a patient to trace lymphatic drainage.
* **Interactive Target:** `immunology-defense-game`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Explain the functional differences between innate and adaptive immunity, and outline how cytotoxic T-cells and helper T-cells clear a viral infection."

### Lesson 3.2: The Respiratory System
* **Concept:** Anatomy of upper and lower respiratory tracts, alveoli structure, physical mechanics of ventilation (Boyle's law, muscle contraction). Gas exchange (Dalton's and Henry's laws). Gas transport dynamics: oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation curve, and shifts in oxygen affinity (Bohr effect: pH, PCO2, temperature, and 2,3-BPG).
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Redrawing the oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation curve by manipulating pH and temperature sliders to simulate exercise acidosis or hypothermia in a clinical unit.
* **Interactive Target:** `bohr-effect-simulator`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Detail the mechanical physics of lung ventilation using Boyle's law, and explain how pH and temperature shifts alter oxygen transport via the Bohr effect."

## Module 2: Digestion, Metabolism & Filtration
*Goal: Understand alimentary canal histology, cellular respiration, and nephron filtration.*

### Lesson 3.3: The Digestive System & Metabolism
* **Concept:** Alimentary canal histological layering (mucosa, submucosa, muscularis externa, serosa), gross digestive organs (mouth to large intestine), accessory organs (liver, pancreas, gallbladder). Metabolism: Cellular respiration pathways (Glycolysis, transition reaction, Krebs citric acid cycle, Electron Transport Chain [ETC]). Digestive enzyme kinetics.
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Matching histological layers of the stomach and duodenum to diagnose peptic ulcer depth, or calculating metabolic ATP yields from glucose breakdown.
* **Interactive Target:** `gi-layering-metabolism`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Detail the four histological layers of the alimentary canal and outline the biochemical pathways of cellular respiration (Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, and ETC), identifying where ATP is generated."

### Lesson 3.4: The Urinary System
* **Concept:** Anatomy of the kidney and nephron (glomerulus, Bowman's capsule, proximal tubule, loop of Henle, distal tubule, collecting duct). Glomerular filtration mechanics: Net Filtration Pressure (NFP) and Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR). Tubular reabsorption and secretion. The Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System (RAAS) activation cascade.
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Calculating net filtration pressures using hydrostatic and oncotic values in a patient with glomerulonephritis, and explaining how renal stenosis triggers RAAS-induced hypertension.
* **Interactive Target:** `glomerular-filtration-calculator`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Describe the structural anatomy of a nephron, the physical forces that regulate glomerular filtration, and the physiological steps of the RAAS pathway."

## Module 3: Homeostatic Balance & Reproduction
*Goal: Master acid-base chemical compensation and reproductive cycles.*

### Lesson 3.5: Fluid, Electrolyte, & Acid-Base Balance
* **Concept:** Fluid compartments (intracellular vs. extracellular), fluid movement. Electrolyte regulation (sodium, potassium, calcium regulation via aldosterone, ANP, PTH). Acid-Base homeostasis: chemical buffers (bicarbonate system), respiratory compensation (hypo/hyperventilation), renal compensation (H+ excretion, HCO3- reabsorption). Link between cardiovascular mechanics and RAAS electrolyte handling.
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Tracing Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) panels to diagnose metabolic acidosis vs. respiratory alkalosis, and identifying active RAAS compensation loops.
* **Interactive Target:** `abg-interpreter`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Explain how chemical buffers, respiratory ventilation, and renal excretion coordinate to maintain blood pH, and how the RAAS pathway regulates electrolyte balance."

### Lesson 3.6: Reproductive Systems & Development
* **Concept:** Anatomy of male and female reproductive systems. Gametogenesis (spermatogenesis vs. oogenesis). Hormonal regulation (GnRH, LH, FSH, testosterone, estrogen, progesterone). Synchronized regulation of the ovarian cycle (follicular, ovulation, luteal) and uterine/menstrual cycle (menstrual, proliferative, secretory). Fertilization, implantation, and early embryonic development.
* **Clinical/Real-World Hook:** Syncing ovarian and uterine phases chronologically on a menstrual calendar, and evaluating infertility caused by hormone imbalances.
* **Interactive Target:** `menstrual-cycle-sync`
* **Feynman Prompt:** "Describe the synchronized hormonal interactions that regulate the ovarian and uterine cycles, and outline the biological phases of gametogenesis."
